ClimaMatch AI — Smart Investment Engine

Overview

Submit startup and investor data. The ClimaMatch AI computes a risk score, an impact score, and suggests investor matches. This is a front-end MVP for demonstration; a real deployment requires a backend, authentication, and secure data storage.

How scoring works

Risk score is influenced by revenue, burn rate, and growth. Impact score combines ESG score and CO2 reduction potential. Inline math: the CO2 impact is modeled as $Impact \approx ESG/10 + CO2/10$ (values normalized to 0–10).
